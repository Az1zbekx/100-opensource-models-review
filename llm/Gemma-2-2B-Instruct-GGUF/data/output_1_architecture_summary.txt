## Event Sourcing vs. Traditional CRUD

| Feature | Event Sourcing | Traditional CRUD |
|---|---|---|
| **State Storage** | Stores a history of all events that led to the current state | Stores a single, immutable snapshot of the current state |
| **Auditability** | Highly auditable, allows tracking of every change | Limited auditability, only records changes made to the current state |
| **Performance Trade-offs** | Potentially higher initial complexity, but can be highly efficient for complex systems | Generally simpler and faster for simple systems, but can be less efficient for complex systems |
| **Complexity** | Higher initial complexity, requires more complex infrastructure and logic | Lower initial complexity, easier to implement and maintain |


**Explanation:**

* **Event Sourcing:** This approach stores a history of all events that have occurred in a system, allowing for a complete and auditable record of every change. This approach is often used in systems where data integrity and traceability are crucial, such as financial transactions or complex software applications. 
* **Traditional CRUD:** This approach focuses on storing a single, immutable snapshot of the data at a given point in time. It is simpler to implement and maintain, but lacks the auditability and history that Event Sourcing provides. 


**Note:** The trade-offs between these approaches depend heavily on the specific application and its requirements.
