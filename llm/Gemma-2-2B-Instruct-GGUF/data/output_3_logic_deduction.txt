Here's how to calculate the probability:

**1. Probability of the first ball being red:**

* There are 3 red balls and 5 total balls.
* Probability of drawing a red ball first: 3/5

**2. Probability of the second ball being red (given the first was red):**

* After taking out one red ball, there are only 2 red balls left and 4 total balls.
* Probability of drawing another red ball: 2/4 = 1/2

**3. Combined Probability:**

* To get the probability of both events happening, we multiply the individual probabilities: 
   (3/5) * (1/2) = 3/10

**Therefore, the probability of drawing two red balls in a row without replacement is 3/10.**
