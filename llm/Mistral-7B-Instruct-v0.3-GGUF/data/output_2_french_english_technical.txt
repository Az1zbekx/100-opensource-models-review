The concept of CQRS (Command Query Responsibility Segregation) in English separates read and write operations to optimize the performance and scalability of distributed databases. This separation allows for the optimization of read-heavy and write-heavy workloads, improving the overall efficiency of the system. By decoupling the concerns of reading and writing, CQRS enables better scalability, as the read and write components can be scaled independently based on their specific demands.

In real-world applications, CQRS can lead to improved response times for read operations, reduced contention on the database, and more efficient resource allocation, making it suitable for high-traffic, data-intensive systems.
