A fintech company charges a 1.5% transaction fee on credit card transactions and 0.8% on debit card transactions. 
However, once a user's cumulative transaction volume in the calendar month exceeds $10,000, a tier discount applies ONLY to the portion of volume exceeding the $10,000 threshold:
- 20% discount on credit card fees (effective fee becomes 1.2%)
- 25% discount on debit card fees (effective fee becomes 0.6%)

Calculate the exact fees for a user who made the following 4 sequential transactions in one month:
1. Credit: $4,000
2. Debit: $5,000
3. Credit: $3,000 (Warning: $1,000 is under the threshold, $2,000 is above the threshold!)
4. Debit: $2,000 (Entirely above the threshold)

Show step-by-step calculations and output the final result as a strict, valid JSON object with the following schema:
```json
{
  "breakdown": [
    {"tx_id": 1, "type": "credit", "amount": 4000.0, "fee": 60.0},
    ...
  ],
  "total_volume": 14000.0,
  "total_fees": ...
}
```
Do not include any conversational filler before or after the JSON.
