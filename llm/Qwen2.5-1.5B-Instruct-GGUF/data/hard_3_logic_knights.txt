Solve this logic deduction problem involving Knights, Knaves, and Spies:
On an island:
- Knights always tell the truth.
- Knaves always lie.
- Spies can either tell the truth or lie.

You meet three people: A, B, and C. You know with certainty that exactly one is a Knight, one is a Knave, and one is a Spy (all three identities are distinct).
They make the following statements:
A says: "I am the Knight."
B says: "A is telling the truth."
C says: "I am not the Knave."

Your task:
1. Determine the exact identity (Knight, Knave, Spy) of A, B, and C.
2. Provide a step-by-step proof testing all potential assignments or using logical deduction to eliminate contradictions.
3. Explicitly explain why C cannot be the Knight, and why A cannot be the Knave.
